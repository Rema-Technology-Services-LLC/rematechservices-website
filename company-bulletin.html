Why teams use it

Stop chasing "is it down?" emails

When something breaks, people want to know what's going on. Company Bulletin gives them one trusted place to look, and tells them the moment anything changes.

Outages, clearly tracked

Set severity and status, post updates, and mark it resolved when it's fixed. A banner on every page shows what's affected right now.

People actually see it

Live updates, desktop pop-ups and email mean nobody has to keep refreshing a page.

Private and in-house

Runs on your own Windows server. Your staff sign in with their Microsoft work account, and your data stays with you.

Features

Everything included

Posts & the feed

  • Four categories: Announcements, Outages, Maintenance and General
  • Filter and search the feed
  • Pin important posts to the top
  • Attach up to 10 photos per post: pick, drag in, or paste a screenshot
  • Photo grid with a full-screen viewer
  • Big phone photos are shrunk automatically, and location data is stripped
  • Live updates: new posts, comments and likes appear without refreshing

Outages & maintenance

  • Set outage severity and status
  • One-click Mark resolved
  • Status banner on every page lists active outages and maintenance
  • "All systems operational" when everything is fine
  • Critical outages stay on screen until dismissed

Conversation

  • Like posts and see who liked them
  • Comment on posts
  • Staff replies shown with a Staff / Admin badge

Notifications

  • Desktop pop-ups for new posts and outage status changes
  • Pop-ups when someone replies to your comment or comments on your post
  • Works even when the site isn't open, in Edge, Chrome and Firefox
  • Email a post to everyone with access, through Microsoft 365
  • Email follow-up updates, such as "resolved", in one click
  • Critical outages are emailed as high importance

Sign-in & roles

  • Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on (SAML)
  • Set up Microsoft sign-in from the Admin page, with no config files
  • Three roles: User, Poster and Admin
  • Roles can come from Entra app roles or groups, or be set by an admin
  • Emergency admin sign-in keeps working when Microsoft is down
  • Posters edit their own posts; admins can edit or remove anything

Admin & branding

  • Your own site name, logo and accent colour
  • Manage users and create local accounts
  • Install and renew the HTTPS certificate in the browser
  • Set up email sending from the Admin page
  • Version & updates page shows when a new release is out
Who can do what

Simple roles

User

Reads the feed, searches, likes, comments and gets notifications. This is everyone by default.

Poster

Everything a User can do, plus creating posts, attaching photos, managing outages, replying as staff and emailing updates.

Admin

Everything, plus editing any post, managing users, branding, certificates, Microsoft sign-in and email setup.

Hosting & security

Runs on your server, under your control

Company Bulletin installs on a Windows server in your network. We can install it for you, or your IT team can do it with a single script.

  • Signed-in access only, including photos
  • Photo uploads checked by content, not just file name
  • Encrypted desktop notifications
  • No third-party scripts or trackers in the app

Requirements

  • Windows Server, running under IIS or as a Windows service with built-in HTTPS
  • Microsoft Entra ID for staff sign-in (recommended)
  • Microsoft 365 mailbox for sending email (optional)
  • Any modern browser, such as Edge, Chrome or Firefox

Easy to keep current

  • One-command updates, with an automatic backup first
  • Roll back to an earlier version if you need to
  • Simple backups: one data folder holds everything
