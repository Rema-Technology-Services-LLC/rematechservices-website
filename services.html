Cloud VoIP with Clearly Cloud

We provide Clearly Cloud from 14IP (formerly ClearlyIP), a complete cloud phone and unified communications system. We handle the setup, number porting and phones, and we're your first call for support.

1 to 10,000users on one system

Calling essentials

  • Auto attendant / IVR: "Press 1 for sales" menus
  • Ring groups and hunt groups
  • Time-based routing for after-hours and holidays
  • Find me / follow me to reach you anywhere
  • Call forwarding, transfer and call waiting
  • Do not disturb
  • Call park and pickup from any phone
  • Call filters and call screening
  • Music on hold
  • Call recording
  • Conference calling
  • Enhanced E911

Voicemail

  • Voicemail to email
  • Record greetings from the web or phone
  • Check and manage messages from any device
  • Custom voicemail options and call flow

Work from anywhere

  • Clearly Anywhere mobile app for iPhone and Android: calls, text and picture messages
  • Desktop softphone: call, voicemail and call history from your computer
  • Business number shown on outbound calls from any device
  • SMS / MMS texting from your business number
  • Use the desk phones you have, or get new ones bundled with service

Meetings & collaboration

  • Video conferencing: two video rooms included
  • Screen sharing, co-browsing and joint annotation
  • One-to-one and group chat with file and link sharing
  • Conference room scheduling and management

Call Panel & call center

  • Call Panel: live view of active calls, users and queues
  • Call queues (ACD) with hold-time estimates and ring strategies
  • Agents pause and unpause with status codes
  • Supervisor monitoring of live calls
  • Queue statistics and wallboards
  • Call detail reports with date ranges and one-click callback
  • Company and personal contacts
  • Easy user management with bulk import and export

Integrations & AI
